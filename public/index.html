    About Soon

    Point Soon at a neglected spot on your block. It reads what kind of place it is, picks one small improvement that would genuinely help, renders it onto your photo, and tells you who to ask.

    Every idea comes from a fixed catalog of real Chicago interventions, each with a researched civic path. Soon will also tell you when a corner already works and needs nothing.

    The fine print

    Your photo is sent to an AI model to draft the idea, and to an image model to render it. Nothing is posted anywhere and nothing is stored on the server. Saved ideas live on this device only.

    Rendered images are AI generated. They are not photographs of anything that exists, and they are not a proposal, permit or promise. Verify every civic path with the city and the ward office before acting on it.
